# Questions to ask the agent

After ingesting `case.json`, run the openjiuwen agent with one of these. The agent reads PPMA
memory through the live bridge; it does not read the file on disk.

## Decision and action review

> For the Helios rollout case, list the decisions, the open actions with owners and due dates, and
> the top risk. Quote the exact PPMA memory statements.

## Owner check

> Which actions belong to Ravi Menon, and what is the earliest due date? Quote the statements.

## Risk check

> What are the open risks for the Helios rollout, and what mitigation was agreed for each?

## Quote lookup

> What exactly was said about the support rota for 8–9 November? Give the quote and the speaker.

## Gap check

> Is there any decision or action in the case that has no owner or no due date? Say plainly if PPMA
> does not hold enough detail.

## Follow-up drafting (review only)

> Draft a short follow-up for Dana that lists the decisions and open actions. Do not send it; return
> the draft for human review.

## Expected shape of a good answer

- It quotes PPMA statements, not invented prose.
- It distinguishes a decision from an action.
- It names owners and dates where PPMA holds them.
- It says “PPMA does not hold that” instead of guessing.
- It never claims to have sent, scheduled or executed anything.
